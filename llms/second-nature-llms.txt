# Second Nature

> Second Nature is a UK-based digital weight-loss programme offering NHS-trusted, CQC-regulated care. It combines weight-loss medication with dietitian-led coaching and behaviour-change support to help people lose weight and keep it off long term.

## Programme

- [How it works](https://www.secondnature.io/how-it-works): How Second Nature's weight-loss programmes combine habit building with support from a registered dietitian or nutritionist to help you keep the weight off for good.
- [Weight-loss medication](https://www.secondnature.io/weight-loss-medication): Compare weight-loss medication options and how each fits Second Nature's clinical programme, with dietitian support and personalised medication reviews.
- [Wegovy pill programme](https://www.secondnature.io/weight-loss-medication/wegovy-pill): A clinical weight-loss programme built around oral semaglutide (the Wegovy pill).
- [Mounjaro programme](https://www.secondnature.io/weight-loss-medication/mounjaro): Mounjaro is a weekly injection that works on two appetite hormones at once.
- [Wegovy programme](https://www.secondnature.io/weight-loss-medication/wegovy): Wegovy is a once-weekly semaglutide injection that reduces hunger.
- [Orlistat programme](https://www.secondnature.io/weight-loss-medication/orlistat): Orlistat is a fat-blocking weight-loss tablet, available over the counter as Alli or on prescription as Xenical.
- [FAQs](https://www.secondnature.io/faqs): Answers to questions about the programme, diet, price, technology, coaching, and cancellations.
